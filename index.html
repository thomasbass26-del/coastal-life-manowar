Two addresses, two buyers

Which one is actually yours

Pine Lakes

Established, walkable, close to the water

Pine Lakes sits between roughly 48th and 62nd Avenues North, less than half a mile from the Atlantic. The streets were laid out around the original 1920s club, so the lots are mature, the tree canopy is real, and you are minutes from the beach, the Market Common, and the airport.

  • Mostly established single-family homes on established lots
  • In-town location, short drive to oceanfront and downtown
  • Renovation and rebuild activity is common here
  • Course is public; club access is arranged through the club directly

Man O' War

Newer build, water views, Carolina Forest corridor

Man O' War is off Highway 501 just west of the Intracoastal Waterway, in the Carolina Forest corridor. Newer construction, wide streets, and a course whose defining feature is water on every hole. If you want a lake or fairway view from a house built in the last few decades, this is the side of town to look.

  • Newer single-family and villa product
  • Lake and fairway views are the premium here
  • Close to Carolina Forest schools, shopping, and Highway 31
  • Course is public; club access is arranged through the club directly
